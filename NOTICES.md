# Asset and library credits

VALORANT weapon and cosmetic images belong to Riot Games. This is an unofficial personal app, unaffiliated with Riot Games.

Catalogue metadata and original image URLs: https://valorant-api.com/ . Bundled snapshot retrieved 18 October 2026. Images were resized proportionally to fit 800 × 400 pixels and encoded as transparent WebP at quality 82; identical output files are deduplicated. Variant renders, swatches, weapon icons and rarity icons are included. No image fetches occur at runtime.

Collection/acquisition metadata and observed-price snapshot: https://valorant.gs/data/ . Its processed data is free to reuse with attribution; underlying game data belongs to Riot. Only observed or returned prices are shown; unavailable prices are not estimated.
